# The look of the two ranges, measured from photographs

Colors are medians of small patches of the photographs, as hex; the date and clock time of each photograph come from its file name. A phone camera took them, so treat each as within about 15 percent of the truth.

**The canyon course (the range at Piru).**
- *The firing line.* A long open-sided shelter: corrugated metal roof on steel posts, concrete floor, green-painted steel benches with round stools, wire-mesh screens between positions. From the bench, the roof's shadow covers the bench and the first yards of ground.
- *The floor.* Sixty to eighty yards of flat, pale tan packed dirt with tire tracks before the first targets; sparse weeds, green in winter and straw in summer; shallow puddles after winter rain.
- *The boards.* The first target line is a long notch cut into a low terrace, about a man's height deep, holding about thirty white boards side by side, each with a blue stenciled number (01 to 30) at its foot and paper targets stapled on, peppered with dark bullet holes. Two groups of four boards (01 to 04 at the left, 05 to 08 at the right) stand nearer on low mounds. A second row farther up carries numbers written 2·01 to 2·30, and a third carries numbers in the 400s (409, 410, 411 are readable), so a board's number appears to begin with its hundreds of yards.
- *The steel.* Red-painted plates: round gongs on single posts and silhouettes (a rectangle with a small square head), each standing in front of a mound of fresh brown dirt, between and behind the rows of boards; the paint is chipped pale grey-white where bullets have struck, and in strong sun the red washes toward pink. Red paint in strong sun, seen through the spotting scope: #af787b. At the right, close in, one round red gong hangs by chains from a blue pipe frame.
- *The canyon.* The floor rises steadily away from the firing line and narrows. The left slope is darker, covered in dense grey-green sage scrub, with a dirt track cut across it. The right slope is grassy with scattered bushes, a dirt road running up along its foot and one large pale boulder. The canyon closes in a steep headwall of bare, pale grey-tan eroded rock scored by vertical gullies, with a track climbing through it and a few lone trees on the ridge. One cluster of trees by the wash at middle distance stays bright green all year.
- *Winter and spring* (12 February 2017, 12:58; 25 March 2017, 12:28 and 15:00): every slope is vivid green grass. Sky #51a4e6 to #4391d9; sunlit grass slope #83965f; scrub or shaded green slope #435044; grass under cloud shade #4b5e3f; bare rock in sun #959990; dirt floor #bdb3a7 to #afa190; weeds on the floor #86896c.
- *Late summer* (11 September 2016, 15:43): the grass is gone to straw and tan, the scrub is dark olive, the floor is paler and dustier. Sky #429ae4; sunlit dry slope #988161; dirt floor #c9bda1; rock in shade #444045.
- *Last light* (the canyon road, 19 November 2016, 16:26): sky #9ac6e9; sunlit dry hills #d1933b; hills in shadow #373432.

**The rifle line (the range at Sylmar).**
- *The firing line.* A roofed shelter on wooden beams with lane signs hanging from the roof's edge (white, black letters and numbers such as D1, D2, D3), concrete benches painted blue-grey with curved cut-outs, round steel stools, hinged wire-mesh screens.
- *The floor.* A wide, flat valley floor of pale grey-tan gravelly dirt, graded into shallow ridges, with wooden beams lying on the ground to mark target lines and target frames of white plastic pipe.
- *The berms.* One long, straight, pale sand berm about twice a man's height across the whole width at the first line; behind it lower terraces step back toward the hills with more target lines; wooden power poles at the right; a band of dark green brush and small trees along a wash behind the berms.
- *The hills.* Rounded and steep, golden dry grass dotted with dark green scrub, white rock showing in the gullies, ridge behind ridge darkening with distance.
- *Autumn afternoon* (21 October 2017, 16:09): sky high up #265bc1, sky near the ridge #4e93e0; sunlit dry slope #b59b6d; berm sand #d1c2ac; turned dirt in low sun #a38867.
- *Spring afternoon* (8 April 2017, 16:11): sky #77b4e8; slope, already drying, #a09681; berm #cac2b7; floor #d1c7b7.
- *Early summer, late afternoon* (17 June 2017, 17:25): sky #8fc5f3; slope #998d77; floor #c5b69f.

**Through glass** (seven photographs taken through a spotting scope at the canyon course). The picture is a circle in a black surround. Its edge has a soft warm brown-orange rim a few percent of the radius wide. The picture is a little softer and lower in contrast than the naked eye's, and strong sun bleaches white boards to pure white. At this magnification the dirt shows as clods and crumbs with hard small shadows, paper targets curl at their corners, and bullet holes show as dark specks.
